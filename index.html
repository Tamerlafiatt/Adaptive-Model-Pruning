02 — Criteria
Selection Criteria

Same three criteria as before — now applied to 1.7M weights across a much deeper input space.

Criterion 1 — Smoothness
$$N_{\text{ext}}^l = \sum_{j} \mathbf{1}_{(w_j - w_{j-1})(w_{j+1} - w_j) < 0}$$
Sign-changes in consecutive differences. On CIFAR-10, avg N_ext is much higher (33–52 vs 3–6 on digits) because the loss landscape is far more complex. Target: small.
Criterion 2 — Jump magnitude
$$S_{\text{ext}}^l = \sum_{\text{extrema}} \max(|\Delta w_j|,|\Delta w_{j+1}|)^2$$
Squared jump size at each oscillation. Higher baseline S_ext on CIFAR-10 reflects noisier gradient updates from a harder task. Target: small.
Criterion 3 — Final magnitude
$$|w_t^l| \qquad \text{score}(w) = \frac{|w_t|}{1 + N_{\text{ext}} + S_{\text{ext}}}$$
Large final value + smooth trajectory = high importance. The combined score is used to rank all 1.7M weights for pruning. Target: large magnitude.

06 — Results Real CIFAR-10
Baseline vs Pruned

41.9% fewer parameters, 2.73× faster inference. The larger accuracy gap vs digits reflects that CIFAR-10 weights are denser and more interdependent.

Pruned 77.0% 0.345 ms 3072→307→153→10 992,075
Δ -4.0% 2.73× faster — −715,199 (−41.9%)
Why the larger accuracy gap on CIFAR-10? On digits (64 features), weights are sparse and pruning ~40% hurts little. On CIFAR-10 (3072 features), the network learns dense, distributed representations across all 1.7M parameters — removing 40% without retraining causes a larger drop. Solution: fine-tune the pruned model for 20–50 epochs to recover most accuracy. The 2.73× speedup is genuine regardless.

Key takeaways
N_EXT 8× HIGHER
CIFAR-10 weights oscillate far more (avg 52 vs 6 extrema). The 3072-dimensional input creates a highly non-convex loss surface, making Adam bounce weights much more before settling.
ONLY −4% ACCURACY COST
With 81% baseline and 77% pruned, the gap is now comparable to MNIST’s ratio. Fine-tuning the pruned model for 20–50 epochs would close it further without retraining from scratch.
2.73× REAL SPEEDUP
The larger architecture (1.7M params) benefits more from structural pruning: removing 715K parameters from 512→307 and 256→153 cuts matrix ops proportionally, giving genuine 2.73× inference speedup.
